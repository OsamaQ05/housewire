# Housewire original game score

All 17 instrumental tracks were newly composed and synthesized for Housewire by the project's audio-generation script. They contain no third-party recordings, melodies, sampled instruments or external downloads. They can be used, modified and distributed with this project without third-party attribution or royalty requirements.

Run `node scripts/generate-game-music.mjs` to regenerate the 22,050 Hz mono PCM WAV files and their technical manifest. Loops are eight bars, approximately 16–27 seconds; notes and room reflections wrap over the loop seam. Individual chapters have separate written arrangements, with shared instrument families reinforcing each room's identity.

After Hours uses mechanical keys, woodblocks and a clockwork music-box finish. Barjeel uses original plucked-string textures (not a claimed traditional composition). The Long Table moves from felt-key warmth to a playful marble tune, a dessert dance, and quieter shadow music. Frequency, Race, Last Light and Forge also have their own scores.

Music is low by default, separately switchable from sound effects, pauses in the background, and yields to microphone capture, voice notes, narrated evidence and informational sound effects. No network connection or account is required for playback.
