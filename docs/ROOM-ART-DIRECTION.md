## Barjeel

Asset: `assets/art/barjeel-cover.png` (1024 × 1536).

Prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: portrait mobile cooperative escape-room cover artwork, no text. Create a sophisticated painterly architectural scene of an old Emirati courtyard house in Dubai, a traditional barjeel wind tower rising above chalky sand plaster walls, a carved dark timber door with an intimate courtyard and geometric window shadows. Theme: the same house remembered across two times, subtly show fresh sunlit wall on one side and weathered indigo evening shade on the other without a literal split-screen or collage. A small wooden optical viewer on a low ledge implies a mystery. Elegant hand-painted illustration with tactile plaster and wood, believable local architecture, inviting not haunted. Palette: warm ivory, deep ink blue, muted palm green, aged brass. Vertical 2:3 composition. Main architecture and door readable in upper two-thirds; lower third quieter dark shadow allowing UI title overlay. No people, no words, no logos, no neon, no generic treasure chest, no stereotyped palace or domes, no watermark.

The setting draws on courtyard houses and wind towers, not a claim that the fictional time puzzle is a cultural tradition.
